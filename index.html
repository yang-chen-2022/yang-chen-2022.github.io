01 / THE CHALLENGE

Failure is not a single event.
It is a story across scales.

From fibre breaks hidden inside a composite to damage developing in a battery electrode, failure begins with local changes and grows into system-wide consequences. We connect those scales to understand — and ultimately anticipate — how materials perform.

02 / WHAT WE STUDY

Different materials.
Shared questions.

We bring fracture and failure mechanics to both load-bearing structures and next-generation functional materials.
