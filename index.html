Materials and structures research

Understanding fracture and failure

We study how materials and structures fracture and fail, combining modelling across scales with experimental characterisation.

Explore our research Yang Chen's Bath research profile

About

Our research centres on fracture and failure in structural and functional materials. We aim to understand the mechanisms that lead to damage and how they influence the behaviour of materials and structures.

We use multiphysics and multiscale modelling alongside experimental characterisation, particularly X-ray computed tomography, to investigate material behaviour.

Research areas

Fibre-reinforced composites

Fracture and failure in structural materials, including damage involving fibres, matrices and their interfaces.

Solid-state batteries

Damage and failure in functional materials, including the interaction of mechanical and electrochemical processes.

Semiconductors

Fracture, defects and reliability in semiconductor materials and structures.

Research methods

Multiphysics modelling

Investigating interactions between physical processes that contribute to material behaviour and failure.

Multiscale modelling

Connecting mechanisms at the microstructural scale with the response of materials and structures.

Experimental characterisation

Using experiments to examine materials and their response to loading and damage.

X-ray computed tomography

Imaging internal material structures and damage in three dimensions.
